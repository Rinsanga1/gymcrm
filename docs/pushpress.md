### What is the best gym management software?

The best gym management software depends on your gym type and stage. For independent gyms running CrossFit, martial arts, strength gyms, or boutique fitness studios, the right platform handles memberships, billing, scheduling, check-ins, and a member app in one system, with transparent payment processing and modular pricing rather than enterprise bundling. PushPress is built specifically for those workflows and is rated 4.8 out of 5 across G2, Capterra, and GetApp from over 196 reviews. Free white-glove migration from Mindbody, Wodify, Zen Planner, GymMaster, Pike13, and Glofox is included.

### How much does gym management software cost?

Gym management software costs range from $0 to $500+ per month depending on features and gym size. PushPress Core Free is $0/month with 4.19% + $0.30 credit card processing. Core Pro is $159/month with 2.89% + $0.30 credit card and 0.79% + $0.30 ACH. Core Max is $229/month with 2.75% + $0.30 credit card and the same ACH rate. Add-on products are priced separately: Grow at $329/month for CRM and marketing, Train starting at $79/month for workout tracking, and the Branded App at $97/month. All plans are month-to-month with no contracts.

### How does PushPress compare to Mindbody, Wodify, and Zen Planner?

PushPress is purpose-built for independent gyms like CrossFit boxes, martial arts academies, strength gyms, and boutique studios. Mindbody is a generalist wellness platform with a dated interface, Zen Planner leans on legacy tech and add-on pricing, and Wodify is CrossFit-only with a thinner CRM. PushPress offers transparent Stripe-based payment processing without hidden surcharges, modular products you can add as you grow rather than enterprise bundles, and a free starting tier. Free white-glove migration from any of those platforms is included.

### What is the best gym management software for small gyms?

Small gyms need a platform that does not require enterprise pricing to access basic operations. PushPress Core Free at $0/month gives new and small gym owners a full operating platform from day one: memberships, billing, scheduling, check-ins, digital waivers, and a member app, with no contract and no setup fee. Most small gyms upgrade to Core Pro at $159/month once monthly revenue passes around $10,000, when the lower processing rates start saving more than the subscription costs.

### Who uses PushPress?

PushPress is built for independent, in-person gym owners running class-based or appointment-based businesses with a physical location. The platform serves over 5,000 fitness businesses across Brazilian jiu jitsu (BJJ), CrossFit, martial arts, Hyrox, yoga, pilates, boxing, functional fitness, small group personal training, and other niche fitness verticals. PushPress is not built for fully remote coaching businesses or big-box gym chains.

### Does PushPress include AI-powered tools?

Yes. PushPress is built around an AI-first approach to gym operations. The PushPress AI Assistant helps gym owners handle quick actions, member follow-up, workout programming, and operations automation. AI tools work across Core for operations, Grow for lead nurture and marketing automation, and Train for workout customization and reporting. The goal is to amplify what coaches and owners do, not replace them.

### Can I switch from another gym management platform to PushPress?

Yes, and migration is free. The PushPress onboarding team imports members, payment plans, billing schedules, class history, and workout records from Mindbody, Wodify, Zen Planner, GymMaster, Pike13, Glofox, and most other gym platforms. Most gyms are fully live within two to three weeks with no disruption to members. There is no setup fee for Core, and white-glove migration support is included.
